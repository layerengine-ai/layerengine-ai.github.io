Section 1

Tier & Snapshot Routing

Every client is routed by their business objective, the approved scope of work, and the selected support tier. Do not promise or deploy a system outside the signed scope. Use the terms below consistently in all client records, task lists, and communications.

Approved Definitions

Tier 1 — Self-Managed

Best for: a client who wants LayerEngine to build the agreed system, then operate it internally after handoff.

What LayerEngine delivers

One approved primary system, the corresponding snapshot foundation, launch assets within the agreed build, and full LayerEngine University access. The client owns and operates the system after handoff. No recurring operational support is included beyond the platform arrangement and any separately contracted work.

Default snapshot route

  • Client Attraction Snapshot: Use for buyer/seller, service-industry, or other approved client-acquisition goals.
  • Brokerage Recruiting Snapshot: Use for a brokerage or office whose approved objective is agent attraction and recruiting.

VA responsibilities during the build

  • Confirm the approved snapshot route and build scope before configuration.
  • Configure documented client-specific values, branding, calendars, required assets, and approved integrations.
  • Complete the relevant snapshot QA checklist and handoff record.
  • Grant or confirm University access and provide the agreed launch materials.

Escalate to owner: any request that adds a second snapshot, changes audience or offer, introduces a new integration, changes AI behavior, alters compliance language, or expands scope.

Tier 2 — Supported

Best for: a client who wants a focused growth build with structured support, but with a deliberately limited ongoing scope.

What LayerEngine delivers

One approved primary snapshot and a complete growth build within the agreed plan, including up to three approved automated workflows. The client receives University access, the defined support cadence, strategic check-in preparation, and approved optimization within the contracted scope.

What the VA may perform

  • All Tier 1 build, QA, and handoff tasks for the approved primary snapshot.
  • Documented optimization and maintenance tasks within the three-workflow / approved operating scope.
  • Prepare performance information and action items for the scheduled strategic check-ins.
  • Triage standard support requests through the approved support channel and escalate scope, strategy, compliance, integration, or AI changes.

Boundary: Tier 2 is not a dual-snapshot deployment, agent-by-agent rollout, unlimited content service, or unlimited custom automation plan. Treat any of those requests as a founder-reviewed scope expansion.

Tier 3 — Fully Managed

Best for: an organization that needs LayerEngine to deploy and operate an approved, full growth infrastructure.

What LayerEngine delivers

Everything in Tier 2, plus the approved full growth infrastructure. Where the client’s operating model requires it, this includes both the Client Attraction Snapshot and Brokerage Recruiting Snapshot, along with a defined agent-enablement or rollout plan. The value is managed operation of the approved system—not unlimited ad hoc administration.

What the VA may build and run within the operating plan

  • Configure and QA both approved snapshots, client-specific values, launch assets, and documented integrations.
  • Run routine system checks, approved workflow/campaign operations, reporting preparation, and documented agent activation steps.
  • Monitor recurring operational tasks and raise deviations, stalled workflows, nonstandard replies, or new requests for founder review.
  • Maintain the client record, version log, support history, and approved change record.

Founder ownership: strategy, exceptions, budget changes, new initiatives, high-risk messaging, nonstandard integrations, major AI prompt changes, commercial scope, and the client relationship. No VA may approve these independently.

Routing rule: Client Attraction is the acquisition system for buyer/seller or other approved client leads. Brokerage Recruiting is the office-growth system for agent attraction. Tier 3 is the only standard tier that may include both systems, and then only within an approved operating plan.

Escalation protocol

  1. Log the request or issue in the approved internal tracking system with client, date, exact request, affected asset/workflow, screenshot or example, and risk level.
  2. Classify it: routine in-scope, missing client input, owner decision, or incident.
  3. For routine in-scope work, complete the documented step, test the result, update the record, and use the approved client communication.
  4. For missing input, request the specific item needed. Do not make up a claim, audience, brand detail, consent language, calendar, phone setting, or integration credential.
  5. For an owner decision, summarize the impact on scope, client outcome, risk, time, and any cost before the owner responds or commits.
  6. For an incident involving unauthorized messaging, apparent compliance risk, account/security issue, live-workflow defect, or unintended public page, preserve evidence and notify the owner immediately. Do not alter or restart a live system unless the owner has approved the action or urgent containment is clearly required.

Service-level rule: Do not invent a response or completion time for a client. Until formal response-time standards are approved, acknowledge the request through the approved channel and obtain owner direction when timing or scope could affect a client expectation.

Snapshot Deployment

LayerEngine uses two approved snapshots: Client Attraction Snapshot and Brokerage Recruiting Snapshot. Deploy the correct system based on the approved client objective and tier. A snapshot transfers reusable configuration; it does not replace client-specific setup, consent, connected accounts, or launch QA.

Choose the correct route

Client objectiveDefault deploymentEscalate when
Attract buyer/seller or other approved service leadsClient Attraction SnapshotThe client requests recruiting, a second system, nonstandard audience, or scope beyond the agreement.
Recruit agents for an office, brokerage, market center, or teamBrokerage Recruiting SnapshotThe client requests buyer/seller acquisition, a second system, per-agent deployment, or nonstandard operations.
Tier 3 approved full growth operationBoth snapshots as defined in the owner-approved operating planBefore loading the second snapshot, before resolving any conflict, or if the agent-enablement architecture is unclear.

Deployment steps

  1. Open the approved source snapshot record and confirm its version, date, included assets, and test status. Do not use an undocumented client account as a source.
  2. For a new subaccount, use the approved primary snapshot during account creation when available. For an existing account, load only the approved assets and review every conflict before selecting override or skip.
  3. For an approved Tier 3 dual-system plan, deploy the second snapshot selectively after the first is present. Record every conflict decision and get owner approval before overriding any live client asset.
  4. Confirm the imported assets match the intended route: relevant funnel(s), form/survey, pipeline, tags, workflows, templates, calendars, custom values, dashboards, and approved AI/knowledge assets.
  5. Keep messaging and public-facing workflows inactive until client-specific customization, consent prerequisites, phone/email/calendar/domain setup, and QA are complete.

What does not arrive automatically: live contacts, appointments, conversations, message history, Stripe/payment connections, third-party integrations, phone-number assignments, and other client-specific connections. These require post-import configuration and verification. Do not treat their absence as a snapshot defect.

Post-Import Customization & QA Checklist

  • Update all approved client-specific values: business/sender name, responsible user, market, calendar, email signature, phone number, domain, offer, and launch assets.
  • Connect and verify the client-specific calendar, sending identity, website/domain, required integrations, and phone configuration.
  • Complete the current A2P/Teli.AI process before any applicable automated messaging goes live.
  • Submit controlled test leads for each live form/funnel and verify tag, pipeline, assignment, internal notification, nurture entry/exit, reply handoff, booking, cancellation/no-show, and opt-out behavior as applicable.
  • Record snapshot version, tester, date, defects, corrections, client approval, and launch result in the activation record.

Snapshot update rule: Snapshot changes do not automatically update existing client accounts. Test changes in an approved test account, version the snapshot, audit live contacts in affected workflows, and obtain owner approval before refreshing or pushing an update to clients.

Section 2

Domain & DNS

Every client's funnel needs to run on their own domain, not a generic GHL URL. This section covers how to connect their GoDaddy domain to their GHL subaccount.

  1. Ask the client for their GoDaddy login credentials (or have them add you as a delegate user).
  2. In GHL subaccount → Settings → Domains → + Add Domain.
  3. Enter the domain (e.g., recruit.johnsmithrealty.com) and click Save.
  4. GHL will display a CNAME record. Copy the Name and Value.
  5. Log in to GoDaddy → DNS Management for the domain.
  6. Add a new CNAME record with the Name and Value from GHL.
  7. Wait 15–60 minutes for DNS propagation. Refresh the GHL domain settings page until it shows Verified.
  8. Assign the domain to the recruiting funnel in GHL.

DNS propagation tip: Use dnschecker.org to monitor when the CNAME record has propagated globally. Do not tell the client their site is live until it shows green in all regions.

Section 2

A2P SMS Registration

A2P (Application-to-Person) registration is required by US carriers before any automated SMS can be sent from a GHL account. Without it, texts will be blocked. This applies to all US-based clients.

What You Need from the Client

  • Legal business name (exactly as registered)
  • EIN (Employer Identification Number) — US clients only
  • Business address
  • Business website URL
  • Sample SMS message they plan to send

Registration Steps

  1. In GHL subaccount → Settings → Phone Numbers → A2P Registration.
  2. Click Register Brand and fill in the business details.
  3. Click Register Campaign — select "Mixed" use case for recruiting/real estate.
  4. Enter the sample message and opt-in language.
  5. Submit. Approval typically takes 3–7 business days.
  6. Once approved, the client's phone number will be enabled for automated SMS.

Canadian clients: A2P registration is not required in Canada, but clients still need a dedicated GHL phone number. Provision one from the Phone Numbers settings page.

For Client Attraction, use the separately approved buyer/seller or service-audience VSL workflow and intake—not this recruiting prompt.

Open the client's GHL contact record. The Notes field contains all their intake form answers. Copy the relevant answers into each [BRACKET] below.

VSL Script Prompt

This is the legacy Brokerage Recruiting VSL prompt. Use it only when the approved system route is the Brokerage Recruiting Snapshot. For Client Attraction, use the approved buyer/seller or service-audience VSL workflow, offer, proof, and CTA; do not recycle recruiting language into a consumer-facing campaign.

You are an expert direct-response copywriter specializing in real estate recruiting. Your job is to write a compelling, authentic VSL (Video Sales Letter) script for a brokerage owner who wants to recruit producing agents. CLIENT PROFILE: - Name: [CLIENT_FIRST_NAME] [CLIENT_LAST_NAME] - Brokerage/Office Name: [BROKERAGE_NAME] - Market/City: [MARKET] - Primary value proposition: [VALUE_PROPOSITION] - Target agent avatar: [AGENT_AVATAR] - Top 3 pain points solved: [PAIN_POINT_1], [PAIN_POINT_2], [PAIN_POINT_3] - Signature offer: [SIGNATURE_OFFER] - Proof points / results: [PROOF_POINTS] - Tone preference: [TONE — e.g., "direct and confident" or "warm and conversational"] TASK: Write a 5–8 minute VSL script (approximately 750–1,200 words when spoken at a natural pace) with the following structure: HOOK (0:00–0:30): Open with a bold, pattern-interrupting statement that speaks directly to the agent's biggest frustration. Do NOT start with "Hi, my name is..." Start with the pain point. PROBLEM (0:30–1:30): Agitate the problem. Why is the agent stuck? What is the industry doing wrong? Make them feel understood. CREDIBILITY (1:30–2:30): Introduce [CLIENT_FIRST_NAME] and [BROKERAGE_NAME]. Establish authority without bragging. Use specific numbers and results. THE THESIS (2:30–4:00): Present the core argument. Why is [BROKERAGE_NAME] the platform that solves the agent's problem? Walk through the value stack. THE OFFER (4:00–5:30): Describe what joining looks like. Be specific about the first 30/60/90 days. Make it feel low-risk and high-reward. THE CLOSE (5:30–end): Clear call to action. Tell them exactly what to do next (click the button below, book a call). Create urgency without being pushy. FORMATTING REQUIREMENTS: - Write in first person as [CLIENT_FIRST_NAME] - Use short sentences and natural spoken language — this will be read on camera - Add [PAUSE] markers where the speaker should pause for emphasis - Add [SMILE] or [LEAN IN] stage direction notes where appropriate - Mark the approximate timestamp at the start of each section - End with a word count estimate The script should feel authentic, not scripted. The agent watching should feel like they are having a real conversation with [CLIENT_FIRST_NAME], not watching an ad.

Pro tip: After generating the script, read it out loud yourself. If any sentence feels unnatural to say, rewrite it. A script that sounds like the client's natural voice converts far better than polished corporate copy.

Automated Conversation System

The automated conversation system is the approved workflow layer that works between human touchpoints. Use the Brokerage Recruiting or Client Attraction path that matches the client’s deployed snapshot; never activate generic outreach without confirming audience, consent, offer, sender identity, and human-handoff rules. It connects the approved lead source, funnel or form, GHL pipeline, and nurture sequence into one monitored system. Your job is to configure the approved version, complete QA, and surface any exception before it reaches the client.

How the Automated Conversation System Works

A prospect sees the approved offer → enters through the correct form or funnel → is created in GHL → receives the configured acknowledgement and nurture → triggers a human-handoff, booking, or qualified reply path at the right moment. The exact sequence depends on the deployed snapshot.

Section 5

Speed to Lead SOP

Speed to lead is the single most important variable in converting inbound leads. Research shows that responding within 5 minutes increases conversion by 9x compared to responding after 30 minutes. This SOP ensures the client's system responds instantly.

Automated Speed-to-Lead Workflow

  1. Lead submits form → GHL contact created instantly.
  2. Workflow fires: immediate SMS sent from client's GHL number: "Hey [First Name], it's [CLIENT_NAME] from [BROKERAGE_NAME]. Thanks for checking us out — I'd love to connect. What's the best time for a quick 20-min call this week?"
  3. Simultaneous email sent (Email #1 from the nurture sequence).
  4. GHL notification sent to client's phone: "New lead: [Lead Name] — [Lead Phone]".
  5. If lead does not reply within 24 hours: Day 1 follow-up SMS fires automatically.

Client instruction: Tell every client to respond personally to leads who reply to the automated SMS within the first hour. The automation opens the door — the client closes it. If they are not responding to replies, the system is wasted.

Section 6

Access & Security

The LayerEngine internal pages (/university and /sop) are protected by Cloudflare Zero Trust. Only approved email addresses can access them. This section covers how to grant and revoke access, and how to troubleshoot login issues.

Cloudflare Zero Trust Access

How It Works

When a user visits layerengine.ai/university or layerengine.ai/sop, Cloudflare intercepts the request and checks if the user's email is on the approved list. If it is, they receive a one-time magic link via email. If it is not, access is denied.

Granting Access to a New Client or VA

  1. Log in to dash.cloudflare.com with the LayerEngine account.
  2. Navigate to Zero Trust → Access → Applications.
  3. Click on the LayerEngine University application (or SOP application).
  4. Click Edit → Policies.
  5. Under the "Allow" policy, add the new email address to the "Emails" list.
  6. Click Save. Access is granted immediately.

SOP page access: The /sop page should only be accessible to the owner and active VAs. Do NOT add client emails to the SOP application. Clients get access to /university only.

Revoking Access

To remove a user's access (e.g., when a client's subscription expires or a VA leaves): follow steps 1–4 above, remove the email from the "Emails" list, and click Save. Access is revoked immediately.

Troubleshooting Login Issues

Issue Fix
"Access denied" error Confirm the email is on the approved list. Check for typos.
Magic link email not received Check spam folder. If not there, resend from the Cloudflare Access login page.
Magic link expired Magic links expire after 30 minutes. Request a new one.
Page shows 404 The HTML file may not be deployed. Check Cloudflare Pages deployment status in the Cloudflare dashboard under Pages → layerengine-ai.

Training Content

Add the following content to the GHL Knowledge Base. Format each entry as a Q&A pair. The more specific the question, the more accurate the AI response will be.

How to Add Content to the Knowledge Base

  1. In GHL Agency → Settings → AI → Knowledge Base.
  2. Click + Add Document.
  3. Paste the Q&A content as plain text or upload a PDF.
  4. Click Save. The AI will index the content within a few minutes.
  5. Test the bot by asking it the questions you just added. Confirm the answers are accurate.

Priority Q&A Entries to Add First

Q: What happens after I pay?
A: Within 24 hours you will receive a welcome email with a link to a short intake form. Once you complete the form, our team will build your custom GHL system and generate your Thesis Presentation and VSL script. Total setup time is 48–72 hours. You will then receive your assets and schedule your onboarding call.
Q: How long does it take to see results from the ads?
A: Most clients see their first leads within 3–7 days of launching their Meta campaign. However, the algorithm needs 7–14 days to optimize. We recommend not making changes to the campaign in the first two weeks. Consistent results typically appear in weeks 3–4.
Q: Can I customize my email sequence?
A: Yes. Your email sequence is fully editable inside your GHL account. Go to Automation → Workflows → [Your Nurture Sequence]. You can edit the subject lines, body copy, and timing of each email. We recommend keeping the structure but personalizing the language to match your voice.
Q: What is a Thesis Presentation and how do I use it?
A: Your Thesis Presentation is a 12-slide deck that makes the logical argument for why a producing agent should join your brokerage. You use it in two ways: (1) as a leave-behind after a strategy call — send the PDF to agents who want to "think about it", and (2) as the foundation for your VSL script. The stronger your Thesis, the higher your conversion rate on calls.

Ongoing maintenance: Update the knowledge base whenever a new FAQ comes up in client support emails. If the same question is asked twice, it belongs in the knowledge base. The goal is for the AI bot to handle 80% of client questions without VA involvement.
