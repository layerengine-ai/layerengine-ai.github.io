1. The "Speed to Lead" Initial Opt-In Workflow

When an agent downloads a resource (like a business plan template) or registers for a webinar, the first 5 minutes are critical. The "Speed to Lead" workflow ensures immediate engagement without requiring a human to lift a finger.

How to Configure It:

This workflow should trigger the moment a form is submitted. The goal is to deliver the promised value instantly while gently initiating a conversation.

  • Minute 0: Send an automated email delivering the lead magnet or registration link.
  • Minute 2: Send a casual SMS: "Hey [Name], just sent over that business plan template to your email. Did it come through okay?"
  • Day 1: If no response, send a follow-up email highlighting a specific tip from the resource.

2. The Long-Term Nurture Sequence

Most agents aren't ready to switch brokerages the day they enter your pipeline. The long-term nurture workflow is designed to keep your brokerage top-of-mind by consistently delivering value over several months.

Touchpoint Timing Content Focus
Email 1 Day 3 Case Study: How an agent increased production by 30% after joining.
SMS 1 Day 7 Quick value add: "Just published a new guide on generating seller leads. Want the link?"
Email 2 Day 14 Behind the scenes: A look at your brokerage's tech stack and support systems.
Ringless Voicemail Day 30 Personal invitation from the broker to grab coffee or hop on a quick Zoom call.

3. The Appointment Booking & Reminder Workflow

Getting an agent to book a meeting is a massive win, but no-shows can kill your momentum. GoHighLevel's calendar workflows can practically eliminate ghosting by keeping the prospect engaged leading up to the call.

Pro Tip: Implement a Pre-Call Questionnaire. Use GHL forms to ask 3-4 qualifying questions immediately after booking. This not only prepares you for the conversation but also increases the prospect's commitment to showing up.

The Reminder Cadence:

Set up automated reminders that build anticipation rather than just stating the time.

  • Immediately: Confirmation email with calendar invite and a brief video from the broker.
  • 24 Hours Before: Email reminder with an agenda for the call.
  • 1 Hour Before: SMS reminder: "Looking forward to our chat in an hour! Here's the Zoom link: [Link]"

4. The Post-Meeting "Hot Lead" Workflow

After a successful recruiting presentation, the follow-up must be flawless. If you leave it to memory, top producers will slip through the cracks. This workflow ensures consistent follow-up until they sign the independent contractor agreement.

"Before we implemented automated workflows, we were losing great agents simply because we forgot to follow up after the first meeting. Now, the system handles the follow-up, and our managers just step in when the agent is ready to sign."

— Sarah Jenkins, Broker/Owner

Configure this workflow to trigger when a contact's pipeline stage is moved to "Meeting Completed." It should send a recap email immediately, followed by strategic check-ins via SMS and email over the next 14 days, offering to answer questions or introduce them to current team members.

Building a Scalable Recruiting Machine

Setting up these GoHighLevel workflows transforms recruiting from an unpredictable grind into a scalable, predictable system. By automating the repetitive tasks, your leadership team can focus their energy on building relationships and closing the best talent in your market.

Yes, GoHighLevel is a comprehensive all-in-one platform that includes CRM functionality, email marketing, SMS automation, funnel building, and more. Many brokerages consolidate their tech stack by moving entirely to GHL, saving thousands of dollars annually on disparate software subscriptions.

How long does it take to see results from an automated recruiting pipeline?
